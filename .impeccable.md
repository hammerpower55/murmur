## Design Context

### Users
People who dictate all day on a Snapdragon X laptop or an Apple Silicon Mac: emails, chat, code comments, often mixing English with Roman Urdu. Murmur mostly lives in the tray; the main window is opened to check history, tune settings, pick a model or glance at insights, usually for under a minute, between other work.

### Brand Personality
Bold, exact, alive. A Swiss concert poster that happens to be useful: huge type, flat colour, one big idea. It notices you (the disc swells with your voice and the rings spin while you dictate) without getting in your way.

### Aesthetic Direction
Swiss poster, visual first, on a dark default.
- One family: Hanken Grotesk, bundled. Page titles are extra bold (800), very tight and very large (96 px), the number a page is about larger still (128 px). Hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from a second typeface, capitals or decoration. Everything is flush left, in sentence case.
- One axis: a 12 column grid. Section headings hang in the margin (3 columns) and content starts on column 4, on every page. Under 44rem the margin column folds away.
- One motif: the amber full stop from the "m." mark. At text size it ends every title and empty state, rides the sidebar rule as the nav marker and sits in the switches. At poster size it is a disc giving off concentric arcs of sound (`Rings`, a seeded picture per page, so the pages read as a series). The only round shapes are the full stop, its rings and the discs on the model map. Everything else has square corners.
- Colour fields: every page opens with a full bleed masthead, plain, inverted (`tone-invert`) or amber (`tone-amber`). A field redefines the colour variables, so anything inside it adapts by itself. Dark is the default, with Light and Match system in Settings.
- Data is the imagery: real numbers drawn big. Thirty days of bars along the foot of Home, one bar per dictation across History, flat colour tiles on Insights, top words set at the size of their count, a speed and accuracy map on Models, date numerals in the margin. No decoration that does not come from the user's data or the motif.
- Rules instead of containers: 1 px ink lines open sections and draw themselves across, hairlines separate rows. No cards, no shadows, no gradients, no blur. The overlay is a flat ink rectangle with flat bars and keeps a small shadow only so it stands apart from other apps.
- Three actions: solid ink for the one thing to do, a ruled box for a secondary action, an underlined word for everything quiet.
- Copy rules from CLAUDE.md apply: sentence case, one clean line, no subtext, no dashes, no exclamation marks.
- Anti-references: rounded cards, soft shadows, glassmorphism, gradient text, side stripe borders, neon glow, all caps labels.

### Design Principles
1. The poster comes first: each page should read from across the room, then reward a closer look.
2. Your words and numbers are the picture: draw real data big before reaching for decoration.
3. The grid decides: if something does not sit on the axis, it needs a reason.
4. Motion is choreography and never a wait: titles rise from behind their baseline, the full stop lands last, rings swing into place and turn with the scroll, rules draw across, bars grow from their baseline, the old page lifts away, a new theme spreads from the control that asked. Nothing blocks a click. Exponential ease out, no bounce, transform and opacity wherever possible, and nothing runs while idle.
5. Respect the body: reduced motion turns every animation into an instant change.
