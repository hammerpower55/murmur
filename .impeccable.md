## Design Context

### Users
People who dictate all day on a Snapdragon X laptop: emails, chat, code comments, often mixing English with Roman Urdu. Murmur mostly lives in the tray; the main window is opened to check history, tune settings, pick a model or glance at insights, usually for under a minute, between other work.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, warm, attentive. Like a good pen: there when you need it, gentle and unhurried, never asking for attention. It notices you (it reacts when you dictate) without performing.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark only (v1). Warm tinted neutrals rather than cold greys, lit surfaces with a soft top highlight and layered shadow so cards sit above the page.
- One accent: the amber of the app icon (oklch about 0.82 0.15 80), used sparingly for live and selected states: the nav marker, toggles that are on, focus, progress, the dictation wave, today's bar. Teal from the icon is not used in the UI.
- Motion is expressive but never in the way: confident entrances, a sliding nav marker, numbers that count up, charts that grow in, a sidebar wave that comes alive while recording. Exponential ease out, no bounce, transform and opacity only, everything off under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Copy rules from CLAUDE.md apply: sentence case, one clean line, no subtext, no dashes, no exclamation marks.
- Anti-references: glowing neon on black, glassmorphism, gradient text, side stripe borders, confetti.

### Design Principles
1. Stay out of the way: no animation delays an action; feedback lands within 100 ms.
2. Warmth through restraint: warm neutrals and one rare amber accent.
3. Motion explains: entrances show what is new, the nav marker shows where you are, the wave shows that Murmur is listening.
4. Depth without decoration: light from above and soft shadows, not glows or blur.
5. Respect the body: reduced motion turns every animation into an instant change.
