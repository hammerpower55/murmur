## Design Context

### Users
People who dictate all day on a Snapdragon X laptop or an Apple Silicon Mac: emails, chat, code comments, often mixing English with Roman Urdu. Murmur mostly lives in the tray; the main window is opened to check history, tune settings, pick a model or glance at insights, usually for under a minute, between other work.

### Brand Personality
Bold, warm, alive. Big confident type on soft rounded shapes. It notices you (the orb stirs and swells with your voice) without getting in your way.

### Aesthetic Direction
Bold and rounded, visual first, on a dark default.
- One family: Hanken Grotesk, bundled. Page titles are extra bold (800), very tight and very large (96 px), the number a page is about larger still (128 px). Hierarchy comes from size and weight, never from a second typeface or capitals. Everything is flush left, in sentence case.
- One axis: a 12 column grid. Section headings hang in the margin (3 columns) and content starts on column 4, on every page. Under 44rem the margin column folds away.
- Rounded everywhere: big panels (28 px), smaller things inside them (12 px), pills for everything you press or toggle, round knobs and soft topped bars. Hairlines separate rows inside a panel. No sharp corners.
- Panels: every page opens with a big rounded masthead, plain, inverted (`tone-invert`) or amber (`tone-amber`). A tone redefines the colour variables, so anything inside it adapts by itself. Dark is the default, with Light and Match system in Settings.
- Data is the imagery: real numbers drawn big. Thirty days of bars along the foot of Home, one bar per dictation across History, colour tiles on Insights, top words set at the size of their count, a speed and accuracy map on Models, date numerals in the margin. No decoration that does not come from the user's data.
- Amber is the one accent, from the "m." mark: the full stop after every title, today's bar, progress, on states, one big panel per page at most.
- The voice orb is the one place with a colour gradient: a dark ball of LED dots in the overlay, built from the user's orb colour. All its motion happens inside it or to it; it never glows or throws light on what is behind it. Anti-references: a lit 3D sphere ("clay dough"), a soft glowing gradient.
- Three actions: a solid ink pill for the one thing to do, a soft pill for a secondary action, an underlined word for everything quiet.
- Copy rules from CLAUDE.md apply: sentence case, one clean line, no subtext, no dashes, no exclamation marks.
- Anti-references: circular or ring animations on page changes (the user tried them and hated them), heavy shadows, glassmorphism, gradient text, side stripe borders, neon glow, all caps labels.

### Design Principles
1. Bold type does the work: each page should read from across the room.
2. Your words and numbers are the picture: draw real data big before reaching for decoration.
3. The grid decides: if something does not sit on the axis, it needs a reason.
4. Motion is smooth and never a wait: pages fade through with nothing changing size or place, titles rise from behind their baseline, the nav pill slides, bars grow from their baseline. Nothing blocks a click. Exponential ease out, no bounce, transform and opacity wherever possible, and nothing runs while idle.
5. Respect the body: reduced motion turns every animation into an instant change.
